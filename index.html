Tool Summary

What GloWoCo Does

GloWoCo is the Global Workforce Compliance Tool. It gives global and in-country teams one place to understand workforce compliance requirements by region, country, and legal entity. It combines jurisdiction guidance, entity-level compliance status, and decision support for hiring, termination, mobility, performance, and local employment obligations.

What it covers

  • Role-based access across global, regional, and country teams.
  • Regional footprint visibility across current operations and potential acquisition countries.
  • Country-level guidance for labor code summary, statutory benefits, hiring, termination, visa process, global mobility, and performance management.
  • Entity-level visibility across core WiseTech entities, acquired entities, and target acquisition entities.

How teams use it

  • People and Legal teams review country rules and required process steps before decisions are made.
  • Country teams assess compliance gaps by uploading proofs, updating statuses, and capturing comments against mandatory requirements.
  • M&A, Legal, and integration teams run workforce due diligence for acquisition targets using target facts, triggered workstreams, and risk outputs.
  • Leadership gets a compact regional dashboard with footprint, entity mix, risk markers, and status signals.

Assessment model

The tool supports two dedicated assessment modes. Compliance Gap Analysis helps owned entities assess ongoing compliance across six core categories using bare minimum requirements, mandatory records, and document proofs. Acquisition Review helps teams assess a target company by capturing target facts first, generating country-specific diligence workstreams, and then evaluating evidence, red flags, and remediation needs.

Why it matters

This tool is designed to reduce fragmented country knowledge, make entity-level compliance more transparent, and help teams move from static guidance to structured assessment. It is a decision-support and governance layer for workforce compliance, not a substitute for current local legal validation or controlled source-data ownership.
